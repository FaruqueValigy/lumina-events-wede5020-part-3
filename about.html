About Lumina Events

Lumina Events is a professional event planning company dedicated to creating memorable experiences for every client. We specialise in weddings, birthdays, corporate functions, baby showers, graduations and private celebrations.

Our experienced planners work closely with clients from the first consultation to the final event, ensuring every detail is carefully managed. We believe every celebration should reflect the client's vision while remaining stress-free and enjoyable.

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional events through creativity, professionalism, attention to detail and outstanding customer service.
